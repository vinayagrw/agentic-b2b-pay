3. Agent-to-API micropayments

Problem

Agents need to pay per call for APIs and data, without accounts or keys.

Root cause

Payment access is built around human accounts. x402 gates access by payment over HTTP 402 instead.

Dollar value

About $24M over 30 days to mid August 2026. About 4,000 wallets drive 90% of spend.

Status today

A Linux Foundation project with 50+ members. Volume is down from its 2025 highs.

Sources: C274, C275, C286, C287, C314.

Real but narrow. Trackers disagree on totals, from 120M to 187M transactions.
