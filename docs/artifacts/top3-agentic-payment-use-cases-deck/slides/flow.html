Sources: C276, C279, C319, C346, C354, C373, C374, C377, C378, C379, C397, C399, C404. Our view: who catches each fail.

Seven checks on one payment. The agent drafts the mandate and a person signs it. Mandate limits on amount, currency and expiry are enforced by rules, as Stripe tokens and Rain cards already do. The payee guard holds any changed bank details, the failure behind the Baltimore loss. The AI risk score only flags: Ramp says early users of its AP agents flagged over $1M of fraudulent invoices in 90 days. Release to a new payee or above a set amount goes to a person under the release matrix, the maker-checker pattern JPMorganChase is building. RTP and FedNow now take up to $10M per payment. Reconciliation is agent work, with a person clearing exceptions.
