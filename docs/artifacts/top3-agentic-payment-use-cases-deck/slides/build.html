Sources: C341, C354, C363, C364, C402. Generic view. Our view: the layers and the two rules.

Five layers, top to bottom along the payment path. The edge checks who the agent is. Visa TAP and Mastercard Agent Pay both use Web Bot Auth over RFC 9421, so one signature check serves both. The decide layer holds the payee guard, the AI risk score and the release matrix, where a rule or a person releases. The stablecoin lane sits behind the orchestrator and stays off until the GENIUS Act takes effect on 18 Jan 2027 and the separate Treasury AML rule is final. In our demo build the chat approval, the bank-change check, the mandate-bound virtual card, the dispute evidence record, early pay terms, the tariff cash scenario, a simulated credit line with maker-checker and copilot charts are built on sandbox or simulated money. Chat approval is a mock preview. Duty rates in the tariff scenario are user inputs with no live feed. The card issuer shows as Stripe Issuing (test mode) or Simulated issuer. The fence on the right is the hard part of the design: the agent proposes, rules decide.
