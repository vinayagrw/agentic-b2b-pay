Ranked by money at risk. The payer loses most: under UCC 4A-202 it is bound when the bank followed the agreed procedure, and FBI-reported BEC losses were $3.05B in 2025. The supplier goes unpaid while courts split. The bank carries the loss only when its procedure was not followed, and it must run Nacha fraud monitoring. Networks leave most losses with merchants and issuers. Agent platforms carry little today, and the Visa pilot sets no liability framework. The gap is that the party with the most power to fix, the bank, is not the one losing most. That is why the payer must own its payee data and mandates.
