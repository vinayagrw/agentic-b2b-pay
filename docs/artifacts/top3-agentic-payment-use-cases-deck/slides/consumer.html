2. Consumer agent checkout

Problem

An agent buys the wrong thing, or more than intended. Some merchants block agents.

Root cause

Reg E treats a payment as authorized once an agent holds your details. Card disputes were built for humans.

Dollar value

No sized market yet. Merchant chargeback costs: $36.9B in 2026, a vendor forecast. Stripe reports 38x growth, no baseline.

Status today

ChatGPT Wallet launched 13 Nov 2026: the buyer approves in chat, the merchant’s own checkout takes payment. US only, Pro tier. Fees and dispute rules not stated.

Sources: C278, C296, C297, C299, C300, C310, C311, C312, C313, W1 (Reported).

Biggest audience, least settled rules. The merchant usually carries the loss today. ChatGPT Wallet, reported by ppc.land on 14 Nov and in the OpenAI commerce guides, uses a token tied to one transaction that must not be saved or reused. It runs on Amex, Mastercard and Visa. Merchant of record, fees and dispute rules are not stated. Shopify and Google, which began its US rollout on 15 October 2026, are also live.
