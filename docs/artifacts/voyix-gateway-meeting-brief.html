What he is likely dealing with

  1. Reliability under fuel volume. Third-party trackers report a Secure Pay incident of about 19 days and a 6.6-hour Voyix Connect outage on 21 Apr 2026. Pilot's and Rutter's volume raises the stakes.
  2. Legacy consolidation competing with the roadmap. Two gateway and token generations, the 2027 Europe/APAC launch, and a certification for every processor and fleet network. Voyix isn't visibly hiring gateway engineers, so delivery capacity is the likely constraint.
  3. Pressure to show AI and agentic commerce. Stripe, Adyen and Worldpay have shipped checkout for AI agents. Voyix is hiring a PM for Agentic Commerce and an Agentic Migration Engineer (payments/POS background, GCP and Vertex AI). Its public AI use covers migration and developer tools, not routing or fraud.
  4. Competition in fuel. Toast Fuel launched around 29 Sep and requires Toast processing. Gilbarco Passport and Verifone Commander already serve the forecourt.

Discovery questions

  1. What does the target architecture look like after Secure Pay and JetPay are retired, and on what timeline?
  2. What is setting the 2027 Europe/APAC date: acquirer certifications, local card schemes, or team capacity?
  3. How long does a new processor or fleet-network certification take today, and how much of it is manual testing?
  4. How is the gateway preparing for Pilot-scale fuel volume: offline authorizations, pump pre-authorization, store-and-forward?
  5. Where is AI already in your engineering lifecycle, and where has it disappointed you?
  6. Is the agentic migration work aimed at POS configuration or at the gateway code itself?
  7. Who owns forecourt integrations: your team, the convenience and fuel group, or partners?
  8. Do you route between processors today, and do you track authorization rates by processor?
  9. Is there interest in AI-driven fraud detection or smart routing for card-not-present and loyalty traffic?
  10. How do you work with offshore and partner teams today, and what has worked?
  11. What would make a 90-day pilot a clear success for you and Darren Wilson?
  12. Is the gateway expected to accept payments started by AI agents, and when?
