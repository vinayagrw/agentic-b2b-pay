## 2. Short profiles

**Ramp.** Target: mid market and enterprise finance teams. What it builds: corporate cards, bill pay, procurement and travel, with AI agents for policy checks, purchasing and AI token spend. How it is built: its own card program, ERP sync with NetSuite, QuickBooks, Sage Intacct and others, plus a public API. Customers: more than 85k businesses (V). Status: $1.85B round led by Dragoneer at $60B, Oct 2026 (V, Ramp press release). Source: https://forkast.news/ramps-reported-60b-round-shows-corporate-spend-software-turning-into-an-agent-platform/

**Bridge (Stripe).** Target: fintechs and platforms. What it builds: stablecoin orchestration, Open Issuance for custom stablecoins, and virtual accounts. How it is built: APIs on top of Stripe, multi chain, USDC and USDB (U). Status: owned by Stripe, $1.1B deal closed Feb 2025 (V). Source: https://www.crossmint.com/learn/bridge-alternatives-for-stablecoin-infrastructure

**BVNK.** Target: enterprises and PSPs. What it builds: stablecoin accounts, payins, payouts and treasury. How it is built: API platform connecting fiat and stablecoin rails. Earlier raise: $50M Series B led by Haun Ventures (V). Status: Mastercard agreed in Mar 2026 to buy it for up to $1.8B. Deal closed 3 Aug 2026 (V, Mastercard press release). Source: https://stablecoininsider.org/how-vcs-are-backing-stablecoin-projects-in-2026/

**Rain.** Target: enterprises and fintechs launching stablecoin cards. What it builds: Visa principal member issuing of cards funded by stablecoins, plus treasury. Status: $250M Series C led by ICONIQ at $1.95B, more than $338M raised in total (V). Source: https://www.rain.xyz/resources/rain-raises-250m-series-c-to-scale-stablecoin-powered-payments-infrastructure-for-global-enterprises

**Zero Hash.** Target: brokers, banks and fintechs. What it builds: regulated custody, trading, stablecoin payouts and tokenization. Status: walked away from Mastercard talks valued at $1.5B to $2B, then sought $250M at $1.5B, Jan 2026. May 2026 reports put the target above $1.5B. No close announced. Last closed round $104M at $1B in 2025 (V). Source: https://stablecoininsider.org/zero-hash-review-2026-the-regulated-infrastructure-platform-behind-wall-streets-crypto-ambitions/

**ORO Labs.** Target: enterprise procurement. What it builds: agentic orchestration of intake to pay across SAP, Coupa and Oracle. Status: $100M Series C led by Brighton Park and Goldman Sachs Alternatives, revenue up about 300% (V). Source: https://www.orolabs.ai/newsroom/oro-series-c-announcement

**Crossmint.** Target: developers building agent commerce. What it builds: agent wallets with spend limits and merchant allowlists, stablecoin onramps, virtual cards, and checkout across millions of products (U). How it is built: dual key wallet in a TEE (one key held by the human, one by the agent), x402 support, MCP server (U). Source: https://fintechspecs.com/blog/best-ai-agent-payment-infrastructure-providers/

**Basis Theory.** Target: merchants, PSPs and agent builders. What it builds: card tokenization vault and agent payments. Single API for agents to pay through Visa, Mastercard and Stripe (Aug 2026), payment from a Stripe Link wallet (Sep 2026) (V). Co-founded the Agentic Commerce Consortium (V). Source: https://blog.basistheory.com/september-2026-changelog

**Lithic.** Target: fintechs and platforms. What it builds: card issuing with real time authorization. Its agentic theme is "Authorization Intelligence", proving what an agent was allowed to buy (V). Member of the consortium (V). Source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/lithic-everything-payments-assumes-about-shopping-is-about-to-break/

**Paxos.** Target: banks and enterprises. What it builds: regulated issuing of USDG, PYUSD (for PayPal) and white label stablecoins (U). B2B relevance: the issuer layer under agent settlement. No 2026 agentic product found.

**Catena Labs.** Target: AI native businesses. What it builds: a regulated financial institution for agents, plus the open source Agent Commerce Kit (ACK) for identity and payments (U). Status: $30M Series A led by Acrew and a16z crypto, May 2026, $48M total (V, one source). Source: https://trio.dev/fintechs-building-agentic-commerce-infrastructure/

**Natural.** Target: B2B agent workflows such as logistics, property and procurement payments (U). What it builds: payment rails that let agents pay and get paid, with identity and controls. Status: $30M Series A led by Forerunner, 20 Jul 2026, more than $40M raised in total (V). Source: https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/natural-bags-30m-launches-agentic-payments-infrastructure

**Zip.** Target: enterprise procurement. What it builds: intake to procure orchestration with AI agents for review and approval. Status: Series D+ Oct 2024, total reported at $383M (V, figures conflict). Source: https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/procurement-software-funding-analysis

**Skyfire.** Target: agent builders and merchants or APIs. What it builds: KYA identity, the KYAPay protocol (signed tokens that carry identity and payment), policy controls, and funding by USDC, card, ACH or wire (V, vendor blog). Status: $9.5M seed+ from a16z CSX, Coinbase Ventures and Neuberger Berman (V). Source: https://rye.com/blog/agentic-commerce-startups

**Kite AI.** Target: agent developers. What it builds: Kite AIR, which gives agents verifiable identity (Agent Passport), policy guardrails and stablecoin payments on an EVM L1. Built natively for x402 (U). Status: $18M Series A co-led by PayPal Ventures and General Catalyst, $33M total (V). Source: https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/09/02/kite-raises-usd18m-to-bridge-stablecoin-payments-and-autonomous-agents

**Brex.** Target: startups to enterprise. What it builds: cards, spend, travel and AI agents for expense handling. Status: acquired by Capital One, announced 22 Jan 2026 at $5.15B, closed 7 Apr 2026 (V, Capital One newsroom). 10-Q puts consideration near $4.5B. Source: https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/procurement-software-funding-analysis

**Lio.** Target: enterprise procurement. What it builds: AI agents that run sourcing and purchasing tasks. Status: $30M Series A led by a16z, 5 Mar 2026, $33M total (V). Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lio-raises-30m-series-a-to-bring-agentic-ai-to-enterprise-procurement-302705236.html

**Didero.** Target: manufacturers and distributors. What it builds: agents that handle supplier email, POs and follow ups (V). Status: $30M Series A, Feb 2026 (V). Source: https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/8ee5a-didero-30m-funding-manufacturing-procurement-ai

**Nekuda.** Target: agent builders and merchants. What it builds: a secure agent wallet, card tokens and "agentic mandates" that hold user intent (U). Status: $5M seed with Madrona, Visa Ventures and Amex Ventures (V). Source: https://trio.dev/fintechs-building-agentic-commerce-infrastructure/

**Payman.** Target: developers. What it builds: agents that pay humans and other agents, with policy checks and audit trails (V, vendor blog). Status: no recent raise found. Source: https://fintechspecs.com/blog/best-ai-agent-payment-infrastructure-providers/
